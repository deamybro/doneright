**Topic:** Scalable Distributed Sensor Architectures for Extreme Climate Monitoring

### Core Research Focus
My primary interest is in developing self-healing, low-power sensor mesh architectures capable of continuous telemetry in challenging environments. During my senior year at Eastern Polytechnic University, I spearheaded the deployment of a 24-node environmental sensor array testing latency and consensus across intermittent satellite links.

### Future Goals for Graduate Fellowship
If awarded the Meridian Global Impact Fellowship, I intend to expand this research into autonomous multi-agent systems that autonomously diagnose communication bottlenecks and coordinate emergency telemetry during natural disaster recovery operations.
