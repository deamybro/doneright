# [Demo data] Meridian Global Impact Fellowship 2026-2027

**Host Institution:** The Meridian Institute for Future Science & Technology  
**Program:** Global Impact Graduate Fellowship  
**Award Value:** $35,000 stipend + tuition remission  
**Application Deadline:** November 15, 2026 (23:59 UTC)  

---

### Program Overview
The Meridian Global Impact Fellowship supports outstanding graduate researchers committed to deploying technological solutions to global climate, resilient infrastructure, and intelligent automation challenges. Fellows receive dedicated mentorship, research grants, and direct sponsorship.

---

### Eligibility Criteria
1. Must hold a Bachelor of Science (B.S.) or Master of Science (M.S.) in Engineering, Computer Science, or closely related quantitative field from an accredited institution.
2. Must maintain a cumulative Grade Point Average (GPA) of **3.5 or higher** on a 4.0 scale (or international equivalent).
3. Demonstrated commitment to research in distributed systems, machine learning, or automated monitoring.

---

### Mandatory Document Submission Checklist
Applicants must submit the following materials through the application portal:
1. **Curriculum Vitae (CV) / Resume:** Current, highlighting academic history, research projects, publications, and relevant work experience.
2. **Official or Verified Academic Transcript:** Issued by university registrar showing all coursework and verified cumulative GPA.
3. **Statement of Purpose (1-2 pages):** Detailing research vision, previous technical achievements, and how the fellowship supports career goals.
4. **Confidential Letter of Recommendation:** At least one formal letter from a principal investigator, academic advisor, or technical supervisor attesting to research capability and character. (Must be provided or formally arranged by candidate).

---

### Important Submission Guidelines
- All uploaded documents must be in PDF or standard markdown/text format.
- Incomplete applications lacking mandatory referee verification cannot be evaluated without formal applicant waiver or referee submission.
- Applications received after November 15, 2026 will not be reviewed.
